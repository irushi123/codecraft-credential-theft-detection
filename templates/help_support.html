Help & Support

Frequently asked questions and how to reach us

Frequently Asked Questions

Why was my account locked?

Accounts are automatically locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts, as a security measure. Contact an administrator to have your account unlocked.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Go to the Forgot Password page and enter your registered email. You'll receive a verification code to reset your password.

Why do I see a "risk score" on my login history?

The system analyzes each login attempt (time, location, device) and assigns a risk score to help detect potential credential theft. A high score doesn't always mean something is wrong — it's a signal for administrators to review.

I received a security alert notification. Should I worry?

It means a login attempt on your account was flagged as high-risk. If it wasn't you, change your password immediately and contact an administrator. If it was you, no action is needed — it will be reviewed automatically.

Why can't I register with my personal email?

Only official campus email addresses are accepted for registration, to ensure only verified students and staff can access the system.

How long until I'm automatically logged out?

Sessions expire after 15 minutes of inactivity for security. Simply log in again to continue.
